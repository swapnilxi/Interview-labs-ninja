Get your reminders on your phone

Say a task in To-Do → Today, like “remind me to call mom tomorrow at 5”, and LabNinja turns it into a task with a reminder. This guide shows how to make that reminder reach your phone or computer, and what is still being built.

How a reminder reaches you

Four routes, from “the app is open” to “the app is closed”. The first three work today.

In the app

Done

A reminder card pops up on any LabNinja page at the exact minute, with Done, Snooze and Open.

Device notification

Done Needs you

A real phone or desktop notification while LabNinja is open or in a background tab. Turn it on once per device.

Calendar alarm

Done

Any task with a reminder can be added to your calendar. The calendar rings even when LabNinja is closed.

Push, app closed

Pending

The server sends the reminder even when no LabNinja tab is open. Needs server setup. See What’s pending.

Done

The server sends the reminder via native Web Push (VAPID) even when LabNinja is closed or your phone is locked.

1 · You speakTap the mic and say the task, a day and a time.
2 · It understandsAI splits the note into tasks when you are logged in. Guests get the instant on-device reading.
3 · You check and addEach task shows its date, reminder and priority as chips you can change.
4 · You are remindedIn the app, as a notification, or by your calendar, at that minute.
Why a phone sometimes stays quiet: phones pause web pages that are in the background to save battery, and only a server push can wake them. Until push is set up, use ⋯ → Add to calendar on any task you must not miss.

Set up your device

Do this once on each phone or computer you want reminders on. It takes about a minute.

Android phone (Chrome)Notifications work in the browser. Installing the app is optional but better.
  1. Open LabNinja in Chrome and go to To-Do → Today.
  2. Tap Turn on notifications (in the Reminders card, or the yellow tip under the capture box), then tap Allow when Chrome asks.
  3. Tap Send a test. A “Reminders are working” notification should appear at the top of your screen.
  4. Recommended: install the app. Tap Chrome’s ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen (or Install app), then open LabNinja from its icon. It opens straight to Today.
  5. Recommended: stop Android from putting Chrome to sleep. Go to Settings → Apps → Chrome → Battery and choose Unrestricted. Also check that Settings → Apps → Chrome → Notifications is on.
Android freezes background tabs after a few minutes, so a reminder can arrive late, or only when you reopen the app. You will then see it as a Missed reminder. For time-critical tasks, use ⋯ → Google Calendar or Add to calendar.
iPhone or iPad (iOS 16.4 or later)Apple allows web notifications only for apps added to the Home Screen.
  1. Open LabNinja in Safari. Check your iOS version under Settings → General → About; it must be 16.4 or newer.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow), scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Close Safari and open LabNinja from the new Home Screen icon. Notifications only work from there, not from a Safari tab.
  4. Go to To-Do → Today, tap Turn on notifications and then Allow.
  5. Tap Send a test to confirm.
  6. Optional: open Settings → Notifications → LabNinja and set Banner Style to Persistent, so a reminder stays on screen until you deal with it. Check that no Focus mode is silencing it.
iOS pauses a web app as soon as you switch away from it. Until server push is live, a reminder fires while LabNinja is open on screen. Otherwise it shows as Missed the next time you open it. For anything important, tap ⋯ → Add to calendar. iPhone opens the event in Calendar with its alarm already set.
Computer (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari)Works in a background tab too. Keep one LabNinja tab open.
  1. Open LabNinja and go to To-Do → Today. The Reminders card is on the right.
  2. Click Turn on notifications, then Allow in the browser prompt.
  3. Click Send a test.
  4. No notification? Allow your browser in the operating system.
    macOS: System Settings → Notifications → your browser → Allow notifications, Alert style Alerts.
    Windows: Settings → System → Notifications → turn on your browser, and switch off Do not disturb.
  5. Leave one LabNinja tab open, even minimised or in the background. Reminders fire on the minute.
Shortcut: on the Today screen, Alt/Option + N starts voice capture without touching the mouse.
“Blocked for this site”You tapped Block once. Here is how to undo it.
  1. Chrome or Edge (computer or Android): tap the icon left of the address, then Permissions or Site settings → Notifications → Allow. Reload the page.
  2. Installed Android app: long-press the LabNinja icon → App info → Notifications → on.
  3. iPhone Home Screen app: Settings → Notifications → LabNinja → Allow Notifications.
  4. Safari on Mac: Safari → Settings → Websites → Notifications → set LabNinja to Allow.
  5. Back in LabNinja, the Reminders card should say Off on this device. Tap Turn on notifications once more.

Saying a task

Speak naturally. The day, time and priority become chips you can tap to change. The rest becomes the title.

Remind me to call mom tomorrow at 5 Pay electricity bill on Friday, urgent Stretch in 20 minutes Gym tomorrow morning Dentist on 3 December at 9:30 am Buy milk; call John and then email the landlord

“Morning” means 9:00, “afternoon” 14:00, “evening” 18:00 and “tonight” 20:00. A bare “at 5” means the next 5 o’clock still ahead. “Remind me” with no time means one hour from now, or 9:00 on the day you named. Tap × Not a time when a phrase like “Friday drinks” should stay in the title.

What’s pending

In order of value. The first item is what makes reminders arrive with the app fully closed.

1. Server push for closed apps Pending

The service worker can already show a pushed message, but nothing sends one yet. Steps for the developer:

  1. Generate a key pair once: npx web-push generate-vapid-keys.
  2. Add environment variables. The browser gets NEXT_PUBLIC_VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY. Both backends (backend/.env and Vercel) get VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY, VAPID_SUBJECT=mailto:you@your-domain and a random CRON_SECRET.
  3. Store subscriptions: a push_subscriptions table (user, endpoint, keys) plus POST/DELETE /todo/push/subscription, in FastAPI and in fe-apis, as the twin rule requires.
  4. In the browser, after permission is granted, call registration.pushManager.subscribe({ userVisibleOnly: true, applicationServerKey }) and send the result to the endpoint.
  5. Add a sweep endpoint, POST /todo/push/sweep, protected by CRON_SECRET. It finds tasks whose remind_at has passed and that have no reminder_sent_at, sends each one with pywebpush or web-push (payload {title, body, href, tag}), then sets reminder_sent_at.
  6. Call the sweep every minute. Vercel Cron runs every minute only on paid plans; Hobby is once a day. On Hobby, use an external scheduler such as cron-job.org or a GitHub Actions schedule, accepting delays of up to 5 minutes.
// sw.js already handles this payload (done):
{ "title": "Call mom", "body": "Reminder · Today, 5:00 PM",
  "href": "/ai-todo?tab=today&task=12", "tag": "reminder-12" }

2. Done and Snooze buttons on the notification Pending

Tapping a notification opens the task, which has those buttons. Acting directly from the notification needs push (item 1) and a signed action link, because the worker has no login.

3. Reminders on Quick Daily tasks Pending

Reminders live on the main tasks: Today, Smart To-Do and the voice capture. Quick Daily has its own task list and shows due dates only.

4. Guest reminders stay on one browser Pending

A guest’s tasks live in this browser’s storage, so their reminders fire only there. Log in to have tasks on every device. Moving guest tasks into a new account is not built.

5. Daylight-saving shift on repeats Pending

A repeating task’s reminder moves by whole days, so a daily 9:00 can become 8:00 or 10:00 after a clock change, where clocks change. Edit the time once, or move to time-zone-aware repeats.

Test it in two minutes

Run this after setting up a device.

  1. On Today, tap the mic and say “stretch in 2 minutes”, then tap Add task. It appears under Today with a reminder chip.
  2. Computer: switch to another tab or app. Phone: keep LabNinja open, or the installed app in front.
  3. At the minute you should get a notification. Tap it and LabNinja opens on that task.
  4. Back in the app, the reminder card offers Done, Snooze and Open. Tap Snooze → 10 minutes and the chip moves.
  5. From the task’s ⋯ menu, tap Add to calendar, then lock your phone. Your calendar rings at that time even though LabNinja is closed.
